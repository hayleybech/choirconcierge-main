We've now added full support for links in the rich text editor. Select the text you want to use as the label, then click the link button to add the URL.

# 2025-11-05
## Pitch Pipe Improvements
You can now customise the sounds used for the pitch pipe and piano tool! Open the learning view then select an instrument from the dropdown. Your choice will be saved for later. There are also a few secret options just for fun! Check back throughout the year.

Other changes:
- You can now select enharmonic pitches (e.g. F# vs Gb) when editing a Song.
- Improved the layout of the piano tool.
---
# 2025-11-04
## Imperial/Metric Conversions
You can now switch between imperial and metric when viewing or editing singers' heights! Check out this new feature on singer profiles and riser stacks.
