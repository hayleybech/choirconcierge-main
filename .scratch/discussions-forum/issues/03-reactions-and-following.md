# 03: Reactions and followed topics

**What to build:** Members can react to accessible topics and replies with emoji, follow topics, and return to discussions from a followed-topics list.

**Blocked by:** 02: Topics, replies, and moderation.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Members can add and remove any supported emoji reaction on accessible topics and replies.
- [ ] A member can have only one reaction for a given emoji and target, while different members and emojis remain independent.
- [ ] Members can follow and unfollow accessible topics and view their followed topics.
- [ ] Reaction and follow actions and listings enforce current tenant and category visibility.
- [ ] Feature coverage verifies reaction uniqueness, follow behavior, and access restrictions.
